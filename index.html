Creator publishing assistant

Mind Craft Studio

A desktop workflow for connecting a creator's TikTok account through OAuth and uploading creator-selected short-form video.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy
How it works: the account owner authorizes TikTok, chooses the video and caption, and controls what is uploaded. Mind Craft Studio does not ask for or store a TikTok password.

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Information used

  • Basic TikTok account information returned after OAuth authorization, such as an account identifier and display name.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep an authorized connection working.
  • Videos, captions, hashtags, and settings that you choose to upload.
  • Limited technical information needed to diagnose failed uploads and maintain security.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the account, perform the upload or publishing action you request, show connection status, and troubleshoot technical failures. We do not sell personal information or use TikTok account data for unrelated advertising.

Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok only as needed to use the TikTok API features you authorize. We do not sell or rent account information to third parties.

Storage and deletion

Disconnecting TikTok removes the app's authorization for future API requests. You can also revoke access through TikTok. Local video files remain under your control; remove them from your computer when no longer needed. Request deletion of any retained technical or connection data by disconnecting the service and contacting the app operator through the channel where the app was provided.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
