Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Information used

  • Basic TikTok account information returned after OAuth authorization, such as an account identifier and display information.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep an authorized connection working.
  • Videos, captions, hashtags, and settings that you choose to upload.
  • Limited technical information needed to diagnose failed uploads and maintain security.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the account, perform the upload or publishing action you request, and keep the connection working. It is not used to post content you did not select or approve.

Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok only as needed to use the TikTok API features you authorize. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and deletion

Disconnecting TikTok removes the app's authorization for future API requests. You can also revoke access at any time in TikTok's authorized app settings. Stored tokens and selected upload data are deleted when you disconnect or request deletion.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above indicates the latest version.
